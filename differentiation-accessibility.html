Differentiation & Accessibility

Same core learning. Different ways to reach it.

Edit this wording directly on the page. Changes are saved only in this browser.

Key line

I vary the route into learning without lowering the ambition of the learning itself.

Six levers

  • Entry point: an appropriate place to begin.
  • Scaffolding: the right amount of support, reduced as independence grows.
  • Modality: visual, concrete, symbolic, verbal and digital representations.
  • Pace: sufficient processing and practice time.
  • Challenge: stretch, depth and complexity matched to readiness.
  • Response: different ways to show understanding.

Interview questions

  • How do you differentiate in a mixed-ability class?
  • How does UDL influence your teaching?
  • How do you challenge a high-attaining student while supporting others?
