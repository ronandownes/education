## How to use it

Do not try to memorise a script. Use a term as a retrieval cue: what do I do, why do I do it, who does it help, what evidence do I look for, and what do I change next?

The wall is mapped across Teaching & Learning, Classroom Management, AEN & Inclusion, Differentiation & Accessibility, Assessment / Feedback / Reporting, Planning & Curriculum, Relationships & Wellbeing, and Professional Responsibility.
