07 · PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

Relationships, Pastoral Care & Wellbeing

Students learn best where they feel they belong.

Edit this wording directly on the page. Changes are saved only in this browser.

Key line

Clear expectations and respectful relationships create the safety students need to participate, take risks and recover from mistakes.

In practice

  • Know students as learners and notice changes in engagement.
  • Build trust through fairness, consistency and follow-through.
  • Use restorative conversations rather than unnecessary confrontation.
  • Work with Tutors, Year Heads, pastoral care, AEN/SEN and families where appropriate.
  • Take attendance, wellbeing and belonging seriously as learning issues.

Interview questions

  • How do you build relationships with students?
  • How do you support student wellbeing?
  • What would you do if a student became emotionally overwhelmed?
  • How do you work with the pastoral system in a school?
