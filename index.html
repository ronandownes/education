Learning to Learn

A simple guide to how learning works in my classroom: how we get started, what support looks like, how we think, and how we show progress.

Start here

THE BIG IDEA

Everyone needs a way into the learning.

My job is to create the conditions in which every student can enter the learning, experience success, and then be challenged to move forward.

DIFFERENTIATION

Same core learning. Different ways to reach it.

01

Entry Point

I give students an appropriate place to begin.

02

Scaffolding

I vary the level of support students receive.

03

Modality

I present and explore learning in different ways.

04

Pace

I allow different amounts of processing and practice time.

05

Challenge

I adjust the level of stretch, depth and complexity.

06

Assessment

I vary how students can show what they understand.

HOW WE LEARN

Support first. Independence next.

Think. Students should explain, predict, test, question and justify.

Use support. Worked examples, diagrams, prompts, models and practical experiences help us get moving.

Remove support. A scaffold has done its job when the learner no longer needs it.

Make mistakes safely. Errors are information. We use them to decide what to do next.

Keep moving. Assessment tells us whether to extend, practise, reteach or try another representation.

FOR STUDENTS

What should you expect?

You should know what you are learning.

You should have a way to get started.

You should be challenged at the right level.

You should know what to do when you are stuck.

You should be able to show what you understand.
