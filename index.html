MR DOWNES · EDUCATOR

Learning to Learn

A simple guide to how learning works in my classroom — and the eight domains that shape my practice as an educator.

Explore the eight domains

THE BIG IDEA

Everyone needs a way into the learning.

My job is to create the conditions in which every student can enter the learning, experience success, and then be challenged to move forward.

01 · TEACHING & LEARNING

Students must do the thinking.

I explain and model clearly, but learning becomes secure when students predict, discuss, test, question, practise and justify.

02 · ACCESSIBILITY

Make the learning reachable.

Students should know what they are learning, understand how to begin and have a usable route through the task.

03 · SPECIAL EDUCATIONAL NEEDS

Support participation. Build independence.

Support should respond to the learner in front of me while preserving dignity, ambition and increasing independence.

04 · DIFFERENTIATION

Same core learning. Different ways to reach it.

01

Entry Point

I give students an appropriate place to begin.

02

Scaffolding

I vary the level of support students receive.

03

Modality

I present and explore learning in different ways.

04

Pace

I allow different amounts of processing and practice time.

05

Challenge

I adjust the level of stretch, depth and complexity.

06

Assessment

I vary how students can show what they understand.

05 · ASSESSMENT, FEEDBACK & REPORTING

Evidence tells me what to do next.

Questioning, observation, discussion, mini-whiteboards, practical work and written responses help me decide whether to extend, practise, reteach or change representation.

06 · PLANNING & CURRICULUM

Every lesson belongs to a bigger journey.

I plan from learning intentions and curriculum outcomes, then build coherent steps towards increasing understanding and independence.

07 · RELATIONAL PRACTICE & WELLBEING

Students learn best where they feel they belong.

Clear expectations, respectful relationships and psychological safety create a classroom where students can take intellectual risks and recover from mistakes.

08 · PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY & SCHOOL COMMUNITY

Teaching extends beyond my classroom door.

I collaborate with colleagues, reflect on evidence, develop professionally and contribute to the wider life and responsibilities of the school.

FOR STUDENTS

What should you expect?

You should know what you are learning.

You should have a way to get started.

You should be challenged at the right level.

You should know what to do when you are stuck.

You should be able to show what you understand.
