THE BIG IDEA

Every student needs a way into the learning.

My job is to create the conditions in which students can engage, experience success, take responsibility and then be challenged to move forward.

01 · TEACHING & LEARNING

Students must do the thinking.

I explain and model clearly, but learning becomes secure when students predict, discuss, test, question, practise and justify.

02 · CLASSROOM MANAGEMENT

Hope. Expectation. Trust.

My approach is preventative first: clear routines, calm consistency and strong relationships. When difficulties arise, I intervene proportionately, restore the learning relationship, record persistent patterns, collaborate with the right people and escalate when necessary.

03 · SEN / AEN & INCLUSION

Support participation. Build independence.

Support should respond to the learner in front of me while preserving dignity, ambition and increasing independence through classroom teaching, team teaching, withdrawal and targeted support where appropriate.

05 · ASSESSMENT, FEEDBACK & REPORTING

Evidence tells me what to do next.

Questioning, observation, discussion, mini-whiteboards, practical work, written responses and formal assessment help me decide whether to extend, practise, reteach or change representation — and how to report progress meaningfully.

06 · PLANNING & CURRICULUM

Every lesson belongs to a bigger journey.

I plan from learning intentions and curriculum outcomes, then build coherent sequences towards increasing understanding, independence and appropriate progression pathways.

07 · RELATIONSHIPS, PASTORAL CARE & WELLBEING

Students learn best where they feel they belong.

Clear expectations, respectful relationships and psychological safety create a classroom where students can take intellectual risks, recover from mistakes and know that adults are working together around them.

08 · PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY & SCHOOL COMMUNITY

Teaching extends beyond my classroom door.

I collaborate with colleagues, safeguard students, reflect on evidence, develop professionally and contribute to the wider life and responsibilities of the school.
