Teaching & Learning

Students must do the thinking.

Key line

I explain and model clearly, but learning becomes secure when students predict, discuss, test, question, practise and justify.

What this means in practice

  • Clear learning intentions and success criteria.
  • Explicit explanation and modelling when students need it.
  • Questioning that checks understanding rather than just participation.
  • Worked examples followed by gradual release.
  • Active tasks where students have to reason, communicate and make decisions.
  • Retrieval, practice and feedback used deliberately.

Interview questions

  • Describe your general approach to teaching and learning.
  • How do you engage a mixed-ability class?
  • How do you know students have understood?
  • Give an example of a lesson that worked particularly well.
