Key line

My approach is preventative first: clear routines, calm consistency, strong relationships and proportionate intervention.

Everyday classroom management

  • Meet students with clear expectations and predictable routines.
  • Notice low-level disruption early and intervene without derailing the lesson.
  • Use seating, proximity, questioning and task design strategically.
  • Protect the learning of the whole class while responding to individual need.

When behaviour becomes more complex

  • Stay calm and avoid unnecessary confrontation.
  • Use restorative conversation and re-entry to learning where possible.
  • Record persistent patterns and work with the Year Head, AEN/SEN, pastoral and management structures.
  • Escalate safeguarding or serious behaviour concerns appropriately.

Interview questions

  • What is your general approach to classroom management?
  • How do you deal with low-level disruption?
  • Describe a challenging behaviour situation.
  • How do you manage several different needs in the same room?
