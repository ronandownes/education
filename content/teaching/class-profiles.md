## Use profiles | How should I use a class profile in an interview answer?

A profile gives me a **specific class to think with**. I identify the starting point, choose the learning response, plan support and challenge, gather evidence and adapt; I do not memorise imaginary numbers or present a synthetic profile as a real class.

| Start | Teach | Adapt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting evidence** | **Learning response** | **Check impact** |
| **Barrier or strength** | **Support or challenge** | **Next step** |

**starting point → response → evidence → adapt**
