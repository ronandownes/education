## Principles for third year

Third year is not a race to finish chapters. 

New learning, cumulative retrieval and exam preparation run in parallel. 

The Ordinary and Higher routes share important mathematical connections but differ in pace, expected independence and depth.

## Indicative sequence


| Period | Core work | Ordinary Level pathway | Higher Level pathway |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Aug–Sept | Diagnose retention; map remaining outcomes | Prioritise essential prerequisites and confidence | Rapid audit followed by demanding mixed problems |
| Sept–Nov | Complete major algebra, geometry and functions sequences | Explicit modelling and graduated practice | Synthesis, proof and unfamiliar contexts |
| Dec–Jan | Statistics, probability and integrated applications | Interpretation, communication and reliable method | Critical analysis, multi-stage reasoning and modelling |
| Feb–Mar | Cumulative interleaving | Topic-to-topic connections with visible scaffolds | Timed and untimed non-routine sets; fading scaffolds |
| Apr–May | Targeted revision and assessment response | Close high-value gaps and build independence | Refine precision, strategy selection and resilience |


## Assessment rhythm

- weekly low-stakes cumulative retrieval
- one substantial mixed assessment per half-term
- error coding by concept, representation and strategy rather than mark alone
- short reteaching cycles followed by a fresh check
- explicit reflection on calculator use, written communication and checking
