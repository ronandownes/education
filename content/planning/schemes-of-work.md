## Planning versus plans | What is the difference between Planning & Curriculum and Plans?

**Planning & Curriculum** is the interview page: it explains my decision-making, such as how I plan a year, lesson, TY programme or difficult topic. **Plans** are the actual working artefacts: schemes of work, sequences, timings, assessment points and resources.

| Planning & Curriculum | Plans |
| --- | --- |
| **How I think** | **What I produce** |
| **Why I choose** | **What I sequence** |
| **How I adapt** | **What I record** |

**principles → decisions → written plan → review**

## How to build a plan | What should a good scheme of work contain?

I start with the **curriculum and the students**, then map a coherent sequence with realistic timing. I build in likely misconceptions, learning experiences, assessment points, retrieval and space to adapt when evidence says the class is not ready to move on.

| Start | Sequence | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Curriculum outcomes** | **Units of learning** | **Assessment evidence** |
| **Starting point** | **Methodologies** | **Teacher reflection** |

**curriculum → starting point → sequence → evidence → adapt**
