## Reporting is a professional interpretation of evidence

A useful report separates attainment from effort or behaviour, acknowledges strengths, identifies a meaningful next step and avoids certainty that the evidence cannot support.

## Questions to prepare

- How do you report progress rather than a single score?
- How do you communicate concern without closing down possibility?
- What should be shared at handover, and what should remain confidential?
- How do common assessments support consistency across teachers?
- How do students contribute to reporting on their own learning?
