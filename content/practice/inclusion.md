## Inclusion is the outcome

Inclusion is not merely placing students in the same room. It means meaningful participation, belonging, access to the central learning and evidence of progress.

## Questions to prepare

- How do routines and classroom language communicate belonging?
- How do you make the core idea accessible without simplifying it into meaninglessness?
- When is in-class support preferable to withdrawal, and when can withdrawal be justified?
- How do grouping and level decisions affect identity and opportunity?
- How do you hear student voice without abandoning professional judgement?
