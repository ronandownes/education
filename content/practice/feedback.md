## Feedback must cause useful action

Comments are not automatically feedback. Useful feedback clarifies the goal, identifies the next improvement and gives the learner an opportunity to act.

## Questions to prepare

- When is whole-class feedback more effective than individual marking?
- How do you keep feedback manageable and specific?
- How do students respond, redraft, correct or reassess?
- How does oral feedback remain visible enough to guide future teaching?
- How do you avoid feedback that creates dependency?
