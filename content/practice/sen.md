## Working distinction

This page focuses on identified special educational needs and the teaching implications that follow. Diagnosis never replaces observation of the learner, and support should widen access without lowering the intellectual purpose of the lesson.

## Questions to prepare

- How do you use a Student Support File or professional report responsibly?
- How do you translate a recommendation into subject-specific classroom practice?
- How do you balance common learning with individual access?
- How do you work with the SEN coordinator, support teacher, SNA, family and student?
- How do you monitor whether a support is actually helping?
