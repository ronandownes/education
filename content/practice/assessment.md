## Assessment asks: what does the evidence show?

Assessment includes diagnostic, formative and summative processes. The essential question is whether the evidence is valid enough to guide a decision.

## Questions to prepare

- How do you establish a real starting point?
- Which hinge question would expose the likely misconception?
- How do you distinguish a reading barrier from a mathematical misconception?
- When is a common assessment useful, and what can it conceal?
- What do you do after the mark is recorded?
