## How do you work with colleagues?
**Key line: A student experiences a school, not a collection of isolated classrooms.**

I share information appropriately, collaborate with subject departments, AEN staff, SNAs, year heads and management, and use common approaches where consistency benefits the student. I also value feedback on my own practice.

## How do you contribute beyond your timetable?
My experience includes extracurricular rugby, school-event photography, examination work, digital-resource development and supporting subject and SEN initiatives. I look for contributions that use my skills and meet a real school need.
