## Working distinction

AEN is broader than SEN. On this page the emphasis is on additional needs not adequately described by a diagnosis alone: anxiety, bereavement, disrupted attendance, EAL, literacy or numeracy gaps, disadvantage, school avoidance and temporary barriers to participation.

## Questions to prepare

- How do you respond when a need is significant but not diagnosed?
- How do you avoid turning a temporary barrier into a permanent low expectation?
- How do you coordinate support after absence or disrupted schooling?
- How do relationships, predictability and regulation support access to learning?
- What evidence shows that the additional support can be reduced or changed?
