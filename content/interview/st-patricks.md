## Two-minute opening

| Experience | Practice | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Maths • Science • Physics • CS • Applied Maths** | **Access without lowering expectations • mixed ability • support** | **Comprehensive education • equality of opportunity** |

I would organise this around **Experience, Practice and Fit**. I am a post-primary **Mathematics, Science, Physics, Computer Science and Applied Mathematics teacher** with substantial experience across Junior Cycle, Senior Cycle, LCA and learning support. A consistent thread through my work is making demanding subject content accessible without lowering expectations. I have taught mixed-ability classes, worked in learning-support and withdrawal settings, used technology and concrete materials, and collaborated with SEN/AEN colleagues and wider school-support structures.

What attracts me to St Patrick's is the fit between that experience and a school whose history is built around **comprehensive education and equality of opportunity**. For me, good teaching comes back to three things: **hope, expectation and trust**.

I also see a strong present-day inclusion strand through AEN, Inbhear, L2LP, LCA, mixed-ability provision and the wider student-support system.

## Classroom management

| Prevent | Respond | Restore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Routines • clarity • relationships** | **Calm • proportionate • record/collaborate** | **Return to learning • repair • escalate when needed** |

I would organise this around **Prevent, Respond and Restore**. **Hope · Expectation · Trust.** Prevent first through routines, clarity and relationships. Intervene calmly and proportionately. Restore the relationship and learning. Record persistent patterns. Collaborate with the correct staff. Escalate when the issue requires it.

## SEN / AEN

| Know the learner | Adapt access | Build independence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Support information • AEN staff • SNA** | **Scaffold • visual/concrete • technology • pace/task** | **Keep ambition high • reduce support over time** |

I would organise this around **Know the learner, Adapt access and Build independence**. Start with the learner, not the label. Use support information. Work with AEN staff/SNAs. Adjust access through scaffolding, concrete and visual materials, technology, pace and task design. Keep the core learning and ambition visible. Build independence.
