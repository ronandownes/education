## School values | We are an ETB community college with our own ethos and values. In your own words, what is your understanding of our values, and how would you support them?

| Understand | Demonstrate | Contribute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LCETB • multi-denominational** | **Excellence + care** | **Mathematics department** |
| **Excellence • Care • Equality • Community • Respect** | **High expectations + support** | **Learning support + colleagues** |
| **Whole student • dignity • diversity** | **Differentiation + assessment** | **Home–school partnership** |
| **School community** | **Confidence + independence** | **Wider activities where useful** |

My understanding is that Castletroy College is a **co-educational, multi-denominational ETB school**, guided by five core values: **Excellence in Education, Care, Equality, Community and Respect**. I see those values as a commitment to developing the whole student, while ensuring that everyone is treated with dignity and has a genuine opportunity to succeed.

For me, ethos has to be evident in everyday practice. In Mathematics, I would combine **high expectations** with **appropriate support**: explain ideas clearly, differentiate where needed, check understanding through formative assessment and help students become more confident and independent. I would also want a classroom where students are respected and mistakes are treated as opportunities to learn.

Beyond the classroom, I would support the ethos by collaborating with the Mathematics department, learning-support colleagues and families, and by contributing to wider school activities where I could add value. I would begin by understanding the school's existing priorities and ways of working. **The essential point is that students should experience those five values through what we do, not simply hear us mention them.**

**Recall route:** Understand the school → demonstrate the values in teaching → contribute to the school community.
