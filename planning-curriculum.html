Planning & Curriculum

Every lesson belongs to a bigger journey.

Key line

I plan from curriculum outcomes and learner need, then build coherent steps towards increasing understanding and independence.

In practice

  • Learning intentions and success criteria.
  • Sequences of learning rather than isolated lessons.
  • Curriculum alignment across Junior Cycle, Senior Cycle and LCA.
  • Planning for mixed readiness and different progression pathways.
  • Using assessment evidence to adjust the sequence.

Interview questions

  • How do you plan a unit of work?
  • How do learning intentions influence your teaching?
  • How do you plan for LCA or mixed exam readiness?
  • How do you make sure you cover the course while meeting student needs?
