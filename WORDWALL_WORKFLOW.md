# WordWall Update Workflow

## Master rule

Every new WordWall term must be captured in the structured WordWall and in the newest-first review stream.

## Update sequence

1. Add the term to the correct domain/category in the website WordWall.
2. Add the term to the matching domain/category in the **Edu WordWalls** spreadsheet.
3. Add a row to the **Most Recent** sheet with the addition time, term and domain.
4. Sort **Most Recent** newest to oldest.
5. Update the **Most Recent WordWall** section at the top of the Google Doc **Prompts** so the newest terms are first.

## Important

- Do **not** chronologically sort the eight structured domain sheets; their category columns are intentional.
- The **Most Recent** sheet is the chronological review queue.
- The Google Doc column layout is independent of the ordering rule: one, two or three columns can be used without changing newest-first order.
- When a term already exists in a structured domain, do not create a duplicate there; it can still appear again in the Most Recent review stream when it becomes newly relevant.
