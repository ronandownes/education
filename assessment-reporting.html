Assessment, Feedback & Reporting

Evidence tells me what to do next.

Edit this wording directly on the page. Changes are saved only in this browser.

Key line

Assessment is useful when it changes the next teaching or learning decision.

In practice

  • Questioning, observation and discussion.
  • Mini-whiteboards, exit tickets and short retrieval tasks.
  • Written work, practical work and formal tests.
  • Feedback that identifies what is secure and what to do next.
  • Reporting progress clearly to students, parents and colleagues.
  • Balancing formative assessment with summative requirements.

Interview questions

  • How do you use assessment for learning?
  • How do you give effective feedback?
  • How do you report progress to parents?
  • How do assessment results influence your planning?
