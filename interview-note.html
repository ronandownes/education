ST PATRICK'S COMPREHENSIVE · MATHS & SEN · INTERVIEW NOTE

Hope · Expectation · Trust

Opening / Why me?

Experienced post-primary STEM and Special Education teacher: Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Physics, Science and Computer Science, with experience across Junior Cycle, Senior Cycle, LCA, mixed-ability teaching and learning support.

Why St Patrick's?

Inclusive, mixed-ability and community-oriented school. Strong fit with my experience of AEN, LCA, restorative practice, purposeful digital learning and supporting students through different pathways.

Classroom Management

Key line: My classroom management is built around hope, clear expectations and trust.

Prevent → Intervene → Restore → Record → Collaborate → Escalate proportionately.

SEN / AEN

Know the learner. Use existing information and the Student Support structure. Differentiate access without lowering ambition. Work with SET/SEN colleagues, SNAs, Year Heads and parents where appropriate. Build independence.

Teaching & Learning

Clear learning intentions; explicit explanation and modelling; questioning; guided practice; active student thinking; CRA/visual/digital representations where useful; retrieval and formative checking.

Assessment & Reporting

Use evidence during the lesson to decide what happens next. AfL, questioning, mini-whiteboards, observation, written work and formal assessment. Feedback should tell the student how to improve; reporting should communicate progress clearly.

School Knowledge

  • Code of behaviour / restorative practice / teacher intervention first.
  • AEN provision, mixed ability, L2LP, Inbhear and LCA pathways.
  • ICT is purposeful and school-directed; personal mobile-phone use is restricted.
  • Pastoral care and student support are shared responsibilities.
