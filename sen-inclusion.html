03 · PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

SEN / AEN & Inclusion

Support participation. Build independence.

Edit this wording directly on the page. Changes are saved only in this browser.

Key line

Support should respond to the learner in front of me while preserving dignity, ambition, participation and increasing independence.

Ways I support students

  • Inclusive classroom teaching and accessible routines.
  • Team teaching and collaboration with colleagues.
  • Small-group and individual withdrawal where appropriate.
  • Concrete materials, visual supports and technology.
  • Student Support Files, plans and communication with the wider support team.

Interview questions

  • How do you support a student with autism or ADHD?
  • What is your experience of learning support and withdrawal?
  • How do you work with an SNA or another teacher?
  • How do you balance support with independence?
