## How to prepare

Each domain will be curated towards **four or five canonical answers**, with variations of the same question grouped together rather than memorised separately. Existing questions and evidence remain available during this reorganisation.

**Recall method:** Read the table headings to reconstruct the answer's main stages; use the individual cells as cues. Speak naturally rather than memorising exact wording. Use **STAR/R** when a specific example is requested.

## Distinguish inclusion from differentiation

**Inclusion and SEN/AEN** concern belonging, equity, access, participation, support needs and collaboration with the student-support team.

**Differentiation** is how teaching, challenge, scaffolding, explanations and assessment are adapted to different starting points within a class. They overlap in practice but are not interchangeable.
